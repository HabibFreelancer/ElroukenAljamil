Architecture Elasticsearch dans le Search service
RabbitMQ Events (ListingPublished/Updated/Deactivated)
        ↓
   Consumers (Search.Infrastructure)
        ↓
ElasticsearchRepository → Index ES "listings"
        ↓
ElasticsearchQueryService ← SearchListingsQuery / SuggestQuery / GetFacetsQuery
